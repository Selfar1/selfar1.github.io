YT Upload NS

YT Upload NS is a private automation tool of NeuroSynergy. It uploads recordings of the owner's own Zoom meetings to the owner's own YouTube channel and sets their visibility (private or unlisted) after YouTube finishes processing.

The tool is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

Google data used

  • youtube.upload - to upload video files to the owner's channel.
  • youtube - to check the processing status of uploaded videos and change their visibility.

See the Privacy Policy.
