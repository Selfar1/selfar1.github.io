YTUpload - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

YTUpload is a private tool operated by NeuroSynergy for a single user - its owner. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What data is accessed

  • OAuth tokens that let the tool act on the owner's YouTube channel.
  • Basic information about videos the tool uploads: video ID, processing status, visibility.

How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's own meeting recordings to the owner's YouTube channel and to set their visibility after processing. The tool does not read, analyse or modify any other content of the channel.

Storage and sharing

  • Tokens and an upload log are stored only on the owner's private server, with access restricted to the tool.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Temporary video files are deleted after a successful upload.

YTUpload's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the stored tokens stop working, and the upload log can be deleted on request.
